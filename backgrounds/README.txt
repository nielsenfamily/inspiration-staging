Background photos for Toolkit: Inspiration

Put up to five photos here with these exact names (they're listed in js/config.js):
  library-1.jpg   library-2.jpg   nature-1.jpg   nature-2.jpg   nature-3.jpg

Free photos you can use: unsplash.com or pexels.com (search "library shelves",
"reading room", "forest path", "mountain lake", "morning field"). Download the
"Large" size (about 2400px wide), rename, and upload to this folder on GitHub.

Any missing photo is simply left out of the choices. The app also has painted
backgrounds built in, and Kayla can add her own photos in Settings.

To change a photo later, use a new file name (e.g. library-3.jpg) and update the
list in js/config.js, so devices that saved the old photo pick up the new one.
